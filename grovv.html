Core Features

Everything you need to grow with intention

Grovv is built around one idea: growth should feel purposeful, not pressured. Every feature is designed to help you show up for yourself.

🌱

Intentional Habit Design

Set habits tied to a growth identity, not just a checklist. Choose from 12 life areas and attach a "why" to every habit you track.

🔥

Streak Engine + Growth Grid

A GitHub-style contribution grid shows your consistency over time. Streaks are rewarded, but rest days don't reset your momentum.

🧠

AI Growth Coach

Weekly reflections powered by GPT-4 that analyze your patterns, celebrate wins, and offer gentle nudges when you're drifting.

📊

Wellness Correlations

Log mood, sleep, energy, and hydration. Grovv surfaces connections between habits and how you feel — backed by your own data.

🎯

90-Day Growth Plans

Set a 90-day focus area and let Grovv build a habit stack around it. Progress is visualized with milestone markers.

🤝

Accountability Circles

Invite up to 5 friends into a private growth circle. Share streaks, cheer each other on, and stay accountable together.

App Screens

Every screen crafted with care

Grovv ships with 12 core screens covering every touchpoint in the user journey — from onboarding to deep analytics.

SCREEN 01

Onboarding Flow

Identity-first onboarding. Users choose their growth areas (mind, body, relationships, craft) before creating their first habit.

SCREEN 02

Daily Dashboard

Greet users by name with today's habit checklist, streak count, wellness quick-log, and a motivational micro-quote.

SCREEN 03

Growth Grid

A visual contribution grid (GitHub-style) per habit. Color intensity maps completion levels over the past year.

SCREEN 04

Habit Builder

Create a habit with identity, frequency, reminder time, icon, and a linked "why" statement in under 60 seconds.

SCREEN 05

Wellness Log

Daily mood, sleep, energy, and water log with a slider UI. Entries take under 30 seconds.

SCREEN 06

AI Coach Insights

Weekly AI-generated insight card with habit pattern analysis, streak celebrations, and growth nudges.

SCREEN 07

90-Day Focus Plan

Set a quarterly growth focus area. Grovv builds a habit stack and tracks milestone completion.

SCREEN 08

Analytics Deep Dive

Trend charts, habit-wellness correlations, completion rates, and best-day-of-week analysis for Pro users.

SCREEN 09

Accountability Circle

Private group of up to 5 friends. Share streak updates, send encouragement, and view each other's daily progress.

SCREEN 10

Notifications & Reminders

Smart reminders with adaptive timing based on past check-in behavior. Never at the wrong time.

SCREEN 11

Profile & Identity

User's growth identity card, total streaks earned, badges unlocked, and data export (CSV/JSON).

SCREEN 12

Paywall / Pro Upgrade

Shown on day 3 after value is delivered. Clean, conversion-optimized screen with annual plan prominently featured.
